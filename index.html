About Me

I am Siddarth Dayasagar, a Robotics Engineer driven by the vision of building intelligent systems that will shape the future of space exploration and humanoid robotics.

My work bridges cutting-edge research and practical engineering: from designing life support and power systems for next-generation space stations, to developing humanoid locomotion controllers, AI-driven agents, and autonomous racing systems.

Skilled in ROS 2, C++, Python, AI/ML, and hardware-software integration, I thrive at building full-stack robotics solutions that combine mechanical design, software, and control theory. My goal is to create technologies that are modular, autonomous, and resilient — ready to operate in the most extreme environments.

Beyond engineering, I am passionate about research, open-source development, and collaboration. I believe robotics is not just about solving technical challenges, but also about expanding human potential — from Earth to space.
